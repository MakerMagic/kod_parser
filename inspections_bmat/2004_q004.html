Raw Main Body

The government now insists that all primary schools spend at least one hour per day on
literacy and numeracy work, and stipulate that a substantial part of the time is spent in whole
class teaching. These changes have helped to raise standards in literacy and numeracy.
Therefore, whole class teaching is clearly more beneficial than small group or individual
work.
