Raw Main Body

Since the late 1990s wolves have been seen in the Haute-France region of the Alps.
This places them once again in conflict with the shepherds who farm this region. Due to
the protected status of the European wolf, French farmers are awarded a
compensation payment for the loss of any of their sheep to a wolf. This payment
amounts to considerably more than the livestock value of the animal. In addition to this,
it is very difficult to distinguish between evidence of a wolf attack and dog attack.
France has a population of 8 million dogs; 8000 of these are estimated to be wild
compared to only 200 wolves.
