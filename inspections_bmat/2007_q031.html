Raw Main Body

Athletes who use a heart rate monitor as a training aid need to identify their maximum heart
rate in order to determine their effective training zones.
It should be remembered that any formula used to determine maximum heart rate (MHR) is
only a best guess (based on research) and not a guarantee of the MHR value.
To determine your maximum heart rate (bpm – beats per minute) you could use the
following, which combines the Miller formula with research from Londeree and
Moeschberger.
1 Use the Miller formula of MHR=217-(0.85 x age) to calculate MHR
Subtract 3 beats for elite athletes under 30
2 Add 2 beats for 50 year old elite athletes
Add 4 beats for 55+ year old elite athletes
Use the resulting MHR value for running training
Subtract 3 beats for rowing training
3
Subtract 5 beats for bicycle training
subtract 14 beats for swimming training
