Raw Main Body

I have two bags of marbles. I know that at least one of the bags contains marbles of only one
colour.
I take one marble from the left hand bag, and put it into the right hand bag. I then take a marble
from the right hand bag and put it into the left.
I then tip the marbles out of the right hand bag and see that half the marbles are green and half
of them red.
