Raw Main Body

A proposal to counteract low turnout by voters in elections is to introduce the option of
voting via the Internet. There are a number of practical issues (such as loss of secrecy, fear
of abuse of the system) which mean the proposal would have to be carefully controlled. It
may be undemocratic by favouring some political parties more than others. Those parties
whose voters tend to be young and better educated would be likely to gain more votes than
others.
