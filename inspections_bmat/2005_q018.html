Raw Main Body

“When a judge delivers a judgement in court, dissatisfied defendants do not stand up and
shout abuse at him. They turn instead to a civilised appeals procedure. However,
footballers who disagree with a referee’s decision often act like thugs and scream dissent at
the referee. We should not tolerate such aggressive behaviour in a civilised society. Any
player acting in this way should automatically be banned from the club’s next three games.
This would be to the benefit of the school community, given that children imitate their
heroes and behave similarly in the classroom and the playground.”
