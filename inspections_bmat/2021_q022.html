Raw Main Body

We all know that publicly funded services in the UK such as health care and education are
overstretched. A growing population, and growing expectations of such services, help to
explain the crisis some of them are facing. At the present time, we all have the legal right to
choose to use a publicly funded service or to pay, for example, for a place for a child in a
private school or for private health care. Whatever rights the law gives us, those who can afford
to pay for such services should do so. With less demand on them, essential services could
continue to be provided for those who cannot afford to pay, and those who can afford to pay
can choose the best they can afford.
