Raw Main Body

I am playing a card game with three friends. Each of us has two cards which are placed face up
on the table. The score for a hand is calculated by adding up the value of the two cards and
then doubling the total if the cards are the same suit (denoted by the symbols ♥, ♦, ♣ or ♠).
The highest score wins. My two cards are the 2♠ and 7♥. The other hands are:
• 4♦ and 7♦
• 5♣ and 8♠
• 2♥ and 9♣
I am the last person to play. I can exchange one of my cards with a card from another hand
and I want to finish in the highest position possible.
