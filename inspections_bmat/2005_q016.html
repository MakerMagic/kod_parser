A farmer has an irregularly shaped buried oil tank which is 1m in height and 2m long. In
order to estimate the volume of oil in the tank he has calculated the volume in the tank as a
function of the height of oil. This height is measured by a dipstick which can be inserted
through the filling hole in the top of the tank.












The dipstick is measuring 0.15m so he orders 500 litres of oil. When the delivery driver has
completed the fill, he measured the height again to check that the correct amount has been
delivered.
