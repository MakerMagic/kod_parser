Raw Main Body

There has been a great deal of research into the use of placebos and, whilst it remains unclear why 
they work, the research clearly shows that placebos can have a powerful effect. Placebos present 
in some cases an ideal therapy; they are cheap and have few or no side effects. Those who argue 
that the prescription of a placebo represents a breach of patient-doctor trust should recognise that 
whilst placebos may not contain any active ingredients, a doctor may prescribe one in the genuine 
hope that this will offer their patient a real therapeutic benefit. In the interests of providing the most 
desirable outcomes, it is clear that placebos should be used as a treatment offered by the NHS.
