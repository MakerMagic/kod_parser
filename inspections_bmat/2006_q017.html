Raw Main Body

Mrs Watts took her grandson and nine of his school friends to a local theme park as a
birthday treat. She had saved £125 to spend on rides, the prices of which are shown
below.
MAIN RIDES Apocalypse £9.00
Armageddon £7.50
OTHERS Dodgems £5.00
Helter Skelter £4.50
Carousel £3.50
All the children want to go on one of the main rides. Mrs Watts decided that the children
could have two rides each. If they chose Apocalypse then they had to go on the Carousel
for their other ride. If they chose Armageddon then they could choose between the
Dodgems and Helter Skelter for their second ride. Two chose to go on Apocalypse.
When she had paid all their rides, she found that she had exactly enough left to buy each of
them a 25p packet of bubble gum.
