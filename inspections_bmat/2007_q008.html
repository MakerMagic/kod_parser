Raw Main Body

Many people think that if they – or ‘the experts’ – cannot explain something, it must
therefore be truly paranormal. An amateur archaeologist declares that because he cannot
figure out how the pyramids were built, they must have been constructed by aliens. Feats
such as the bending of spoons, or telepathy, are often thought paranormal or mystical. But
if they were explained people would respond, "Yes, of course" or "That's obvious once you
see it." Fire-walking is a case in point. People speculate about the supernatural powers of
fire-walkers over pain and heat, yet the simple explanation is that the conductivity of heat
from the light and fluffy coals to the walker’s feet is very poor. As long as you don't stand
still, you will not get burned.
