Raw Main Body

The use of animals for experiments for medical research has been criticised by many animal
rights campaigners as morally wrong. Some campaigners use extreme tactics to gain public
attention. But they ignore the many benefits which advances in medicine have for animals
themselves. The development of antibiotics, for example, has helped animals as well as
humans. If the animal population as a whole derives benefit from experiments on only a
small minority of animals, those experiments are morally acceptable.
