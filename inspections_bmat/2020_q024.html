tandems (two-wheeled for two riders) and unicycles (one-wheeled without handlebars). The
number of essential components required for each product is shown in the table below.
product saddles wheels handlebars
bicycle 1 2 1
tricycle 1 3 1
unicycle 1 1 0
tandem 2 2 2
We know that yesterday 10 saddles, 14 wheels and 9 handlebars were used and 7 cycles were
built in total.
