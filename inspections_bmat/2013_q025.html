Raw Main Body

My neighbour has two 24-hour digital clocks in her living room. One of them is always 25 minutes 
fast and the other is always 16 minutes slow. Why she keeps them like this I don't know, but it does 
mean that once every day, for two successive minutes, the eight digits displayed on the two clocks 
are all different.
