Raw Main Body

Many people knowingly buy fake brands because they are substantially cheaper than the
genuine article would be. They may try to justify their behaviour, on the grounds that the
manufacturers and retail outlets are already rich enough from ripping off the public with their
exorbitant prices and paying rock-bottom wages to their employees. As true as these claims
may be, it doesn’t excuse the shopper for encouraging piracy.
