Just four horses start in the 3:15 race, a short sprint. They are: Arctic Ape, Bistro,

ahead of Arctic Ape, but nothing more specific is known. By the half way mark the only
change is that Drumbeat has dropped back one place. Then, in the last furlong, Bistro
moves up one place and that is how they finish.
