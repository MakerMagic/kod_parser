Raw Main Body

Lack of sleep is known to adversely affect our attention, alertness, concentration, judgement and 
problem-solving abilities. Every human faculty deteriorates with age, and the ability to sleep well is 
no exception. Old people have more difficulty falling asleep, and thus on average have less sleep 
than young people. This must account for the impairment in memory which often occurs with 
ageing.
