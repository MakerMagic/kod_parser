Raw Main Body

Jason had 240 Spruggles that he wanted to sell, so he rented a market stall for two days. 
He set the price at £12 each, and was disappointed when he sold less than a quarter of them on 
the first day. 
He decided that he would reduce the price by 25% for the second day. 
On the second day he sold exactly twice as many, and took £342 more than the previous day, 
leaving him with less than one third of his original stock.
