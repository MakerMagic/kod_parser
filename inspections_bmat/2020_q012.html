Raw Main Body

Meg is making costumes for the 30 members of the Rainbow Dancers, for their annual show.
The costumes can be made in a variety of colour combinations, as shown in the following table.
The table also shows the number of dancers who will have each variety of costume.
costume colour number of dancers
100% red 4
100% blue 4
100% yellow 3
100% green 3
50% red, 50% blue 2
50% blue, 50% yellow 2
50% green, 25% red, 25% blue 2
60% red, 20% yellow, 20% blue 10
Each costume can be made from exactly 2m of material. The material comes in 30m rolls and
Meg buys one roll in each of red, yellow, green and blue.
