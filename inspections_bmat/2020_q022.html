Raw Main Body

A belief is a thought about how things are – about what really is or is not the case. For
instance, someone who believes in God believes that a being such as God actually exists.
Assuming that people would prefer to have true beliefs rather than false ones, then they need
to find a way to determine which is which. Unfortunately, people have a tendency to want to
‘protect’ their existing beliefs by only seeking evidence that supports them and being blind to
contrary evidence. This makes for a very poor test of whether a given belief is indeed true.
Actively seeking the evidence that goes against your own beliefs allows you to make a proper
assessment, and consequently a far more reliable judgment.
