Raw Main Body

The Royal Society, Britain’s leading science organisation, has millions of pounds invested in
fossil fuel companies. At the same time, it has published many doom-laden reports on the
impact of human activity on the environment, and many of its members are devoted to
researching climate change. It is hypocritical for the Society’s money to be invested in
companies whose operations are in conflict with many of its publications and the careers of its
members. The Society should abandon its investments in fossil fuels. This would be following
the example of other organisations such as the National Theatre and the Royal Shakespeare
Company.
