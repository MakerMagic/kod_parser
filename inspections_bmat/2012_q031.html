Raw Main Body

In each round of a competition the competitor who performed best in that round is awarded 6
points. The next best competitor gets 3 points and the third gets 1 point. There are four
competitors, so the last player does not score any points. After 10 rounds the scores are
totalled. After nine rounds had been completed, Jill knew that, regardless of the outcome of the
final round there would be no ties and she would finish in third place. She also knew that
whoever finished ahead out of Karen and Gemma would be the overall winner.
