Raw Main Body

Recent research suggests that antiretroviral drugs designed to prevent HIV-positive individuals
developing AIDS also have an effect on multiple sclerosis (MS). A study of 21000 HIV-positive
individuals treated at English hospitals between 1999 and 2011 found that they were 62% less
likely to develop MS compared to non-HIV-positive individuals. One explanation for this could
be that HIV infection actually combats the onset of MS in some way. However, some theories
on the development of MS suggest that it has its origins in a viral infection.
