Raw Main Body

The implementation this year of more random speed traps to prevent accidents is absurd.
Figures published earlier this year showed that only 6% of accidents are caused by speeding.
But half the deaths on the roads are caused by drivers under 25. The road safety campaigners
should turn their attention to finding ways of helping young people to drive safely while they are
gaining experience, instead of advocating the pursuit of safe and responsible drivers for
technical infringements of arbitrary limits.
