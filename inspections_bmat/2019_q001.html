Raw Main Body

The total cost of a flight between two cities is made up of three parts: a basic cost, taxes and a
booking fee. Last year, for any flight, the basic cost and the taxes were in the ratio 3:2 and the
booking fee was fixed at $50. This year, the basic cost of a flight has been increased by 20%,
the taxes have been increased by 10% and the booking fee has been halved.
