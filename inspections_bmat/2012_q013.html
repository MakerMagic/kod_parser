Raw Main Body

The unusual weather the UK has experienced over the past few years - very dry warm springs
and very cold winters - is what one would expect if the melting of Arctic ice were influencing our
weather. And indeed, over the same period the Arctic has undergone its most dramatic
shrinkage of sea ice. This means that the Arctic Ocean is warming, which can upset the global
balance between cold Arctic air and warm tropical air.
