Raw Main Body

During the trial of a 10 year old accused of frequent violent crimes, privacy laws meant that he
could only be referred to in the media as ‘Child B’. One newspaper, however, continually
referred to the boy as the ‘Devil Child’. Despite many media sources reporting the difficult and
violent upbringing the boy himself had had, calling for understanding and leniency, many letters
sent to newspapers and comments posted on internet forums showed strong public anger and
calls for the harshest possible sentence. Almost all of those calling for tough measures used
the term ‘Devil Child’. This shows that the name alone had influenced public opinion and
prevented members of the public having sympathy for the child.
