Raw Main Body

The best way to reverse the trend towards increasing violence amongst the young is to
encourage more young men to take up boxing. Boxing is not primarily about aggression; it
is about discipline and self-restraint. It also provides an activity to keep young men
occupied during their leisure time. In every instance in which a boxing club has been set up
in a high-crime area there has been a reduction in violent crime and drug use.
