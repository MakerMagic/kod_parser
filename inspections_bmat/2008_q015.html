Raw Main Body

Radiation from mobile phones is not strong enough to heat biological tissue or break chemical
bonds in cells. However, it may affect cell behaviour. Researchers exposed ten female
volunteers to radiation at 900MHz from mobile phones to simulate an hour long phone call.
They screened 580 different proteins in their skin cells. They found that the numbers of one
protein increased by 89% in all volunteers. Numbers of a second protein decreased by 32% in
all volunteers. This is the first study to show molecular effects of phone radiation on humans.
