Raw Main Body

A group of ten teenagers attending a party meal are each given a card numbered from 3 to 12 as 
they arrive at the venue. The card is to be used to find their places at the table when it is time for 
the meal. Some of the places at the table already have the numbers showing but the teenagers 
have to solve the problem of where the rest of them should sit in order to meet a list of criteria given 
to them by their host. The partial seating plan is shown below: 
  
The remaining guests must sit in places such that the sum of any four place numbers that fall in a 
straight row makes 29.
