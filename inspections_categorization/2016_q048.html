Main Body

The planet Melpomene has three moons, Othello, Hamlet and Romeo, each moving at a constant speed in the same direction in circular orbits.

Othello takes 20 days to complete one orbit of Melpomene, whereas Hamlet takes 45 days and Romeo takes 120 days.

Main Question

How often are Melpomene and all three of its moons in line with each other in the order shown in the diagram?

Main Answers

A

every 36 days

B

every 72 days

C

every 132 days

D

every 180 days

E

every 216 days

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

We are simply required to find the “phase” (location of moon within period) of each moon at these time intervals and see which has all three moons at the same phase. To get the phase at a certain time, we divide the time by the period. This means that one period (corresponding to a phase of 360 degrees/2*pi radians) has a phase of 1. We only need to consider the phase within a cycle so we can ignore the number before the decimal point. For example, to get the phase of a orbit with period 20 days after 54 day, we first remove any whole periods: 54-20-20=14. This corresponds to ignoring the part before the decimal point. After this we divide by the period to get a phase of 14/20.

We will simply use a decimal number for the phase as the units do not actually matter:

  • Every 36 days: Othello=16/20, Hamlet=36/45 and Romeo=36/120
  • Every 72 days: Othello=12/20, Hamlet=37/45 and Romeo=72/120
  • Every 132 days: Othello=12/20, Hamlet=42/45 and Romeo= 12/120
  • Every 180 days: Othello=0, Hamlet=0 and Romeo=½
  • Every 216 days: Othello=16/20, Hamlet=36/45 and Romeo=96/120

The only answer with all three moons at the same phase (hence will appear collinear) is answer choice E

Raw explanation excerpt

Question 48: E We are simply required to find the “phase” (location of moon within period) of each moon at these time intervals and see which has all three moons at the same phase. To get the phase at a certain time, we divide the time by the period. This means that one period (corresponding to a phase of 360 degrees/2*pi radians) has a phase of 1. We only need to consider the phase within a cycle so we can ignore the number before the decimal point. For example, to get the phase of a orbit with period 20 days after 54 day, we first remove any whole periods: 54-20-20=14. This corresponds to ignoring the part before the decimal point. After this we divide by the period to get a phase of 14/20. We will simply use a decimal number for the phase as the units do not actually matter: ♦ Every 36 days: Othello=16/20, Hamlet=36/45 and Romeo=36/120 ♦ Every 72 days: Othello=12/20, Hamlet=37/45 and Romeo=72/120 ♦ Every 132 days: Othello=12/20, Hamlet=42/45 and Romeo= 12/120 ♦ Every 180 days: Othello=0, Hamlet=0 and Romeo=½ ♦ Every 216 days: Othello=16/20, Hamlet=36/45 and Romeo=96/120 The only answer with all three moons at the same phase (hence will appear collinear) is answer choice E
