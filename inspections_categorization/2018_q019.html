Main Body

A Youth Centre runs a number of football teams for boys of different ages. Each season, one prize is awarded to the player who has given the best all-round performance. Five players have been nominated as possible recipients of this year’s prize. The manager has decided not to reward any player who has missed two consecutive training sessions more than twice, and the manager will also exclude anyone who has failed to score a goal from a penalty kick more than twice. After these criteria have been applied, the prize will go to the player who has scored the highest number of goals.

Main Question

Which player will receive the best all-round performance prize?

D

Final Explanation

First apply both exclusions. David misses 16−13 = 3 penalties and Colin misses 13−9 = 4, so both are excluded. Graham has three instances of missing consecutive training sessions, also excluding him. John and Mike qualify; Mike scores 29 goals against John's 26. Thus Mike, D, wins.

  • A: David's 32 goals do not help because his three missed penalties exceed the permitted two.
  • B: John meets both conditions but scores only 26 goals, fewer than Mike's 29.
  • C: Colin misses four penalties, exceeding the limit despite his 30 goals.
  • E: Graham's 36 goals are highest, but he misses two consecutive sessions three times and is ineligible.
