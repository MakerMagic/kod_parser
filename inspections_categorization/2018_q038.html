Main Body

The diagram below shows part of a map of Ruralia.

A group of students are on an expedition and must meet their teacher at Longwood, grid reference 787634. Unfortunately, they have been given a version of the map with the numbers missing from the gridlines, and the place names have been omitted too. They have a list of grid references for some places on the map, and are trying to work out which point to head for in order to be sure of meeting their teacher.

They have a set of instructions showing how the grid references work: The grid reference of point P is 172427. Go to vertical line to the west of the point, read the number 17. Estimate how many tenths across the square the point appears at, hence 172. Go to horizontal line to the south of point P, read the number 42, estimate how many tenths up the square the point is, hence 427. This gives 172427 as the complete grid reference number. Here is the list of the grid references / place names the students have been given.

Main Question

Using this information to help you, which of the places marked on the map is Longwood?

Final Explanation

Use both whole-square position and tenths within a square. Hilltop (763613) fixes the low-left point at easting 76.3, northing 61.3; the rest of the pattern then locates the grid. Longwood is 78.7 east and 63.4 north. The point W lies about seven tenths across the 78 square and four tenths above line 63, so W, B, is Longwood.

  • A: V is around 77.7/63.4: it has the correct northing but lies one whole square west of Longwood.
  • C: X is approximately 79.2/62.6, matching High Moor rather than Longwood.
  • D: Y is approximately 78.1/61.4, matching Riverside and lying much farther south.
  • E: Z is approximately 80.4/61.3, matching Sunnybank, east and south of Longwood.
