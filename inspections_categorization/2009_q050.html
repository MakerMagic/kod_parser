Final Explanation

The view shown in E) is not a possible view because the Red and Yellow sides are in the wrong positions.

We can see from the view given, that when Blue and Yellow are folded round, they will be positioned such that if we are still looking at Red Face-on, the blue will be on the left behind the red, and the yellow will be on the right behind the red. When we rotate this round to get a top view, we see that if blue is on the bottom, then Yellow must be on the right, and red must be on the left. Thus, View E) is not a possible view. All other views are possible.
