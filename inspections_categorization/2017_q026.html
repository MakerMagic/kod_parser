Main Body

The following net is folded up to make a cube.

Main Question

At how many corners of the cube do exactly two of the diagonal lines on the faces meet?

Main Answers

A

2

B

3

C

4

D

5

E

6

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

Track diagonal endpoints as the six faces fold into a cube: endpoints at the same three-dimensional vertex must be counted together. The folded net gives four vertices with two diagonal endpoints and four vertices with one. This accounts for all twelve endpoints from six face diagonals. Therefore exactly four corners have two meeting diagonals, C.

  • A: Two would omit two of the four distinct folded vertices where a pair of diagonal endpoints meets.
  • B: Three undercounts the four two-diagonal vertices produced by folding the net.
  • D: Five overcounts: each of the other four vertices has only one diagonal, not a pair.
  • E: Six also counts vertices with only one diagonal as though they had two.
