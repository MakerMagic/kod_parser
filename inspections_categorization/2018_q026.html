Final Explanation

Ben looks along the columns of the overhead plan. Its leftmost column has just one stack, in the second row from the top. Amy's view fixes that row's maximum height at six, so Ben's leftmost stack cannot appear seven high. E shows seven on the left and is impossible. The other options place the seven-high maximum in one of the two columns that can contain it.

  • A: Heights 5,6,7 are possible by putting the seven-high stack in the right column and the six-high stack centrally.
  • B: Heights 5,7,6 can place the seven-high stack centrally and the six-high stack on the right.
  • C: Heights 6,5,7 allow the lone left stack to be six high and the seven-high stack on the right.
  • D: Heights 6,7,5 allow six on the left, seven centrally and the mandatory five-high stack on the right.
