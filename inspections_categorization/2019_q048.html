Main Body

Sourdough, an ingredient of many bread products, is a fermented mixture of flour and water. At 8:00am on Monday morning, I start with a mixture of 50g flour and 50g water. Each morning at 8:00am I double the weight of the sourdough by adding equal weights of flour and water. I know that 1g of the water content evaporates every hour. The bread recipe that I would like to use requires 500g of sourdough. I also want at least 50g of sourdough left over so that I can continue growing it for next week’s bread.

Main Question

On which day am I going to have the necessary amount of sourdough?

Main Answers

A

Tuesday

B

Wednesday

C

Thursday

D

Friday

E

Saturday

Correct Answer

D

Final Explanation

The baker needs 500g for the recipe and at least 50g remaining, so the total sourdough required is 550g. The mixture initially weighs 100g on Monday morning. It loses 1g of water per hour, or 24g between daily additions. Each morning, its remaining weight is doubled by adding equal weights of flour and water. Tuesday: (100 − 24) × 2 = 152g. Wednesday: (152 − 24) × 2 = 256g. Thursday: (256 − 24) × 2 = 464g. Friday: (464 − 24) × 2 = 880g. Thursday's amount is insufficient, but Friday's exceeds 550g. Therefore, D is correct.

  • A: Tuesday's mixture weighs only 152g after doubling, which is 398g below the required 550g.
  • B: Wednesday's mixture weighs 256g, still 294g below the minimum required for baking and retaining a starter.
  • C: Thursday's mixture weighs 464g, which is 86g short of the required 550g.
  • E: Saturday is later than necessary because the mixture first reaches the required amount on Friday.
