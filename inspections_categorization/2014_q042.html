Main Question

Correct Answer

Final Explanation

This question asks us to find the value for y, the 5-year moving average for the year of 1909.

We do not know the average maximum day temp for 2009 itself (value x), or next year’s 5-year moving average (value z)

However, we can calculate the value of x, using the 5-year moving average for 1913. This average will be composed of the average of the mean max temps for the Years 1909,1910,1911,1912 and 1913.
