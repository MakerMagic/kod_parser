Main Body

The current 'Special Offer' gimmick at Fareprice involves putting a band marked 'TWINPACK - 25% OFF' around pairs of selected items, such as washing powder.

Main Question

Which one of the following 'Special Offers' is exactly the same as Fareprice's?

Main Answers

A

'Buy one - Get a second for half price.'

B

A larger packet containing 50% extra for no extra cost.

C

'Three for the price of two.'

D

A coupon on each packet which gives 25% off the cost of the next packet.

E

'Five for the price of four.'

Correct Answer

A

Full Explanation

The important word in this question is ‘exactly’. Options C and E can be eliminated because you cannot buy exactly two packets for less than full price. Also, ‘three for the price of two’ is the equivalent of a 33% (1/3) reduction and ‘five for the price of four’ is the equivalent of a 20% reduction. Option B can be eliminated because 50% extra in one packet is only the equivalent of 1 ½ packets. Also, it is the equivalent of a 33% reduction. Option D can be eliminated because only one coupon could be used on the first two packets bought, so the reduction would only be 12.5% on the total cost of the two packets. Option A gives a reduction of 25% per packet. The correct answer is option A.
