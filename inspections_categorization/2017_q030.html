Main Body

There are two ways of scoring points in a ball game: a ‘major’ scores 5 points and a ‘minor’ scores 3 points. In a match played yesterday, the Reds beat the Blues 77–52 despite the fact that the Blues scored exactly twice as many majors as minors, whilst the Reds scored exactly half as many majors as minors.

Main Question

How many majors were scored altogether in yesterday’s match?

Main Answers

9

B

12

C

15

D

18

E

21

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

A major scores 5 points and a minor scores 3. The Blues scored twice as many majors as minors. Let their number of minors be b, so their 52 points equal 5(2b) + 3b = 13b. Thus b = 4 and the Blues scored 8 majors. The Reds scored half as many majors as minors. Let their majors be r, giving 77 = 5r + 3(2r) = 11r. Thus r = 7 majors. Total majors = 8 + 7 = 15. Therefore, C is correct.

  • A: 9 majors is 6 fewer than the established combined total of 15 and cannot satisfy both teams' scores and scoring ratios.
  • B: 12 majors is 3 fewer than the required total. The Blues' 8 majors and Reds' 7 majors already establish 15.
  • D: 18 majors exceeds the calculated combined total by 3 and is inconsistent with the stated scoring constraints.
  • E: 21 majors exceeds the combined total by 6. Neither team's major count can increase without violating its point total or major-to-minor ratio.
