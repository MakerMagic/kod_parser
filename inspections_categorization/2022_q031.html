Main Body

The table shows the cost of hot drinks in a local café.

Belle bought a number of hot drinks, including at least one of each tea, coffee and chocolate. She was charged £32.00.

Main Question

If she bought the same number of teas as coffees, what was the maximum number of hot drinks that Belle could have bought for the £32.00?

Main Answers

A

9

B

10

C

12

D

13

E

14

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

Belle bought the same number of teas and coffees, and at least one chocolate. 1 tea and 1 coffee cost £1.50 + £2.50 = £4.00. 1 chocolate costs £3.00. First, buy one of each: £4.00 for the tea and coffee pair and £3.00 for the chocolate. This totals £7.00, leaving £25.00. Now, £25.00 divided by £4.00 (for each pair of tea and coffee) equals 6 sets of tea and coffee pairs. That gives 6 teas and 6 coffees, which totals 12 drinks. Add in the 1 chocolate and the 1 initial set of tea and coffee, and we get 12 + 2 = 14 hot drinks.
