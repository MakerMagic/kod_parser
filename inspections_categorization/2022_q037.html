Main Body

In a ball game points are accumulated by different combinations of four winning shots called a chop, a creamer, a glink and a yip. Playing the game for the first time, the only knowledge I have about the scoring system is that a chop is worth one point more than a creamer, and a creamer is worth twice as many points as a glink. I lose narrowly by 47 points to 50, scoring 3 chops, 4 yips, 3 creamers and 4 glinks. My victorious opponent scored 5 chops, 3 yips, 3 creamers and 2 glinks.

Main Question

Which one of the following statements about the scoring system is the only one that is true?

Main Answers

A

A creamer is worth more points than a chop.

B

Two chops are worth less than three yips.

C

A glink is worth 2 points.

D

Three yips are worth more than three creamers.

E

A chop is worth 6 points.

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

You scored 3 chops, 4 yips, 3 creamers, and 4 glinks, totaling 47 points. Your opponent scored 5 chops, 3 yips, 3 creamers, and 2 glinks, totaling 50 points. The difference between you and your opponent’s score is:

(5 chops - 3 chops) + (3 yips - 4 yips) + (3 creamers - 3 creamers) + (2 glinks - 4 glinks) = 2 chops - 1 yip - 2 glinks = 50 - 47 = 3 points. We know: A chop is worth 1 point more than a creamer. A creamer is worth 2x the points of a glink.

Let’s assume the value of a glink is 2 points:

  • Then, a creamer would be worth 4 points (2x2).
  • A chop would be worth 5 points (4+1).

Your score would be: 3x5(chops) + 4xY(yips) + 3x4(creamers) + 4x2(glinks) = 47 15 + 4Y + 12 + 8 = 47 4Y = 12 Y = 3 (points for a yip) Your opponent’s score would be: 5x5(chops) + 3x3(yips) + 3x4(creamers) + 2x2(glinks) = 50 25 + 9 + 12 + 4 = 50 50 = 50 Both equations match the total points. Thus, C; A glink is worth 2 points; is correct.
