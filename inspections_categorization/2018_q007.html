Main Body

Baytree Island’s Council is concerned to retain a balance of skilled professional workers amongst the population, and monitors migrants in and migrants out on an annual basis. There is currently some concern about the trend, and the Chief Administration Officer was asked to prepare a statistical table for the next Council meeting, as shown below:

Main Question

In which year did the island have the greatest overall loss of professionals?

Main Answers

A

2013

B

2014

C

2015

D

2016

E

2017

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

Use the professionals' P column, since P counts all persons. Net loss equals professionals leaving minus professionals arriving. The losses for 2013–2017 are respectively 51−59 = −8, 50−55 = −5, 68−44 = 24, 67−45 = 22 and 65−44 = 21. The largest loss is 24 in 2015, so C is correct.

  • A: In 2013, 59 professionals arrive and 51 leave: a gain of 8 rather than a loss.
  • B: In 2014, 55 arrive and 50 leave, giving a net gain of 5.
  • D: The 2016 loss is 67−45 = 22, smaller than the loss of 24 in 2015.
  • E: The 2017 loss is 65−44 = 21, also smaller than the 2015 loss.
