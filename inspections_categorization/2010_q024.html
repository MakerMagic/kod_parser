Main Body

A secretary is in a bad mood with her boss and has to write three letters intended for three different people. She makes sure that each of the recipients of the letters will receive one written to someone else.

Main Question

In how many different ways can she send three different letters to achieve this?

Main Answers

A

1

B

2

C

3

D

5

E

6

Correct Answer

B

Final Explanation

It is easy to calculate the possible distributions of letters in this instance. Let the three intended letter recipients be termed X, Y and Z:

If the letter intended for person X is sent to person Y, then the letter for person Y must be sent to person Z, and the letter for person Z be sent to person X. If the letter intended for person X is sent to person Z, then the letter for person Y must be sent to person X, and the letter for person X be sent to person Y.

This accounts for all possible ways of ensuring everybody receives a letter intended for someone else.
