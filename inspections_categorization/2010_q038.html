Final Explanation

If the shapes are examined, we see that they all correspond to the orientations of the different symbols as shown in the 3 views in the question, apart from the die in E).

The Die in E) shows arrow pointing away from the X, which is the correct orientation, as seen in the left-hand view of the question. However, the crescent in Die E) is in the wrong orientation. We can see in the question that the Arrow points toward the face with the two parallel lines, which both lie perpendicular to the direction of the arrow. We can also see that one end of the 2 parallel lines point towards the face with the crescent on, such that they point towards the concave side of the crescent.

Thus, we can see that one of the tips of the crescent must point towards the face with the arrow on. In Die E), it is the concave side of the crescent that faces toward the arrow. Thus, Die E) is not the same as the one in the question. Hence, the answer is E).
