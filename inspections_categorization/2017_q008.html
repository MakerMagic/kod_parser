Main Body

Rosie needs to mow her lawn. She has just bought a new lawn mower, and believes she can mow at a rate of 2m2 a minute. With her old mower, she could only mow the lawn at a rate of 1m2 a minute. Her lawn is 10m by 25m. There is a patio in one corner which is 2m by 3m and which she does not need to mow.

Main Question

How much time does she save with her new lawn mower?

Main Answers

A

122 minutes

B

125 minutes

C

128 minutes

D

244 minutes

E

250 minutes

Correct Answer

A

Final Explanation

The mowing time depends on the area of grass, excluding the patio. The lawn covers 10 × 25 = 250 m², while the patio covers 2 × 3 = 6 m². Therefore, the area requiring mowing is 250 − 6 = 244 m². With the old mower, Rosie needs 244 ÷ 1 = 244 minutes. With the new mower, she needs 244 ÷ 2 = 122 minutes. The time saved is 244 − 122 = 122 minutes. Therefore, A is correct.

  • B: 125 minutes is the time saving that would result from treating the entire 250 m² lawn as grass. The 6 m² patio must be excluded.
  • C: 128 minutes exceeds the established saving by 6 minutes and is inconsistent with the 244 m² of grass and the two stated mowing rates.
  • D: 244 minutes is the total time needed with the old mower, not the difference between the old and new mowing times.
  • E: 250 is the numerical value of the entire lawn's area in square metres, before excluding the patio. It is not the number of minutes saved.
