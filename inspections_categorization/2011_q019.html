Final Explanation

A) is the view looking at the logo from the right-hand side. We can see the 3 points of the “E” in the Logo, which are on the left, middle and right in this view.

C) is the view looking at the logo from the bottom. We can see the bottom point of the “T” in the centre, with gaps either side, as expected. On the left and right are the wide bases of the “J” and “E”.
