Main Body

The following ingredient and nutrition information (rounded to the nearest 0.1g or 1kcal) appears on a 300g packet of oatcakes. Each oatcake provides:

Ingredients: oatmeal (77%), vegetable oils, wheat flour, sugar, salt, raising agent: sodium hydrogen carbonate. Typical values per 100g: energy 1792kJ, 427kcal; protein 10.7g; carbohydrate 56.45g of which sugars 2.4g, starch 53.7g; fat 17.6g of which saturates 5.0g, mono-unsaturates 7.9g, polyunsaturates 4.5g; fibre 8.9g; salt 1.4g of which sodium 0.6g.

How many oatcakes are there in a packet?

Final Explanation

Use energy to compare a whole 300 g packet with one oatcake. At 427 kcal per 100 g, the packet contains about 3 × 427 = 1281 kcal. Each oatcake supplies 53 kcal, so 1281 ÷ 53 ≈ 24.17. The figures are rounded, and the only compatible option is 24, D. The fat figures independently give 3 × 17.6 ÷ 2.2 = 24.

  • A: Seven oatcakes at 53 kcal supply only about 371 kcal, far below the packet's roughly 1281 kcal.
  • B: Eight oatcakes supply about 424 kcal, approximately the energy per 100 g rather than per 300 g packet.
  • C: Twenty-one oatcakes supply about 1113 kcal, too little to be explained by rounding.
  • E: Twenty-seven oatcakes supply about 1431 kcal, well above the packet total.
