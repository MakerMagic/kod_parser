Main Body

A digital display consists of seven segments which light up in different combinations to produce digits. For example, to display the digit ‘1’ the two right-hand segments light up as shown:

The display is used to show, in sequence, each of the digits 1 to 9.

Main Question

What is the maximum and minimum number of times that any one segment lights up during this sequence?

Main Answers

A

7 4

B

7 5

C

8 3

D

8 5

E

9 4

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

Count how often each fixed segment is used by digits 1–9; do not count the total segments in a single digit. The lower-right segment appears in every digit except 2, so its count is 8. The lower-left appears only in 2, 6 and 8, giving 3. The other segment counts lie between these limits. Thus the maximum/minimum pair is 8 and 3, C.

  • A: The maximum exceeds 7 because the lower-right segment lights for eight digits; the minimum is 3 rather than 4.
  • B: Both limits are wrong: the lower-right reaches 8 and the lower-left is used only 3 times, not 5.
  • D: Its maximum of 8 is correct, but the minimum of 5 overlooks the sparsely used lower-left segment.
  • E: No segment appears in all nine digits; even the lower-right is off for 2, and the minimum is below 4.
