Main Body

The drawing below shows an allotment shed.

[diagram not to scale] The four outside wood surfaces of the shed, including the door, need to be painted with three coats of paint. It takes 4 minutes to paint 1 square metre. I have two identical sheds to paint and will take just one 20-minute tea break.

Main Question

How long will it take to paint the two sheds?

Main Answers

A

36 minutes

B

1 hour and 48 minutes

C

2 hours and 8 minutes

D

3 hours and 36 minutes

E

3 hours and 56 minutes

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

One shed has front and back areas 2.5×1 and 2×1, plus two trapezoidal sides each averaging(2.5+2)/2×1=2.25 m². Total wooden area is 9 m²; the metal roof is excluded and the door included. Two sheds, three coats and 4 minutes/m² take 9×2×3×4=216 minutes. Add the single 20-minute break:236 minutes, or 3 hours 56 minutes, E.

  • A: 36 minutes covers just one coat on one shed:9×4. It omits the other coats, second shed and break.
  • B: 108 minutes covers three coats on one shed only, omitting the second shed and tea break.
  • C: 128 minutes is 108 plus the break, still painting only one shed.
  • D: 216 minutes is the correct painting time for both sheds but omits the required 20-minute break.
