Main Body

A school party consisting of 4 teachers and 20 students is visiting the theatre. Of the students, 8 are aged 15 years and 12 are aged 16 years. The following table shows the entry prices to the theatre.

Main Question

What is the least amount that the school party must pay for entry to the theatre?

Main Answers

A

$525

B

$530

C

$545

D

$550

E

$560

Correct Answer

A

Final Explanation

The twelve 16-year-olds count as adults, giving 16 adults and 8 children altogether. Split them into three groups of eight, distributing the children 2, 2, 4 and adults 6, 6, 4. Each group meets the at-least-two-children condition and costs$175, so the total is 3×$175=$525, A. Comparing the other packages cannot improve this: even replacing one eligible group with two full family tickets costs$180.

  • B: $530 is achievable with two$175 groups and two$90 family tickets, but three groups save another$5.
  • C: $545 is above the feasible$525 total; no extra charge is required for the 16-year-olds once grouped.
  • D: $550 can buy two$175 groups and a$200 supergroup, but the third group can also satisfy the cheaper child condition.
  • E: $560 also exceeds the verified minimum; the available eight children are sufficient to qualify all three groups.
