Peter has drawn two diagrams showing information about the two cars owned by his parents:

Main Question

Which one of the following statements is not correct?

Main Answers

A

The more expensive car is newer.

B

The cheaper car is bigger.

C

The older car is slower.

D

The newer car is bigger.

E

The slower car is smaller.

Correct Answer

B

Final Explanation

The first plot shows car 2 is more expensive and newer than car 1. The second shows car 2 is bigger and faster. Hence the cheaper car, car 1, is smaller, so B's claim that it is bigger is the one incorrect statement.

  • A: This is true: car 2 has the higher market price and the lower age.
  • C: This is true: car 1 is older on the first plot and has the lower maximum speed on the second.
  • D: This is true: newer car 2 is the larger car according to the size axis.
  • E: This is true: slower car 1 is also smaller on the second plot.
