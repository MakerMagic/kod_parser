Main Body

Iman and Maya ran a 100 metre race. Iman started 2 seconds after Maya and finished 3 seconds before her.

Main Question

If each girl ran at a constant speed throughout the race, and Iman’s speed was 25% faster than Maya’s, for how many seconds did Iman run?

Main Answers

A

15 seconds

B

18 seconds

C

20 seconds

D

22 seconds

E

25 seconds

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

20 seconds. Let Maya’s speed be x metres per second. Then Iman’ s speed is 1.25 times x. The total time taken for Maya to complete the 100 metre race would be 100 divided by x seconds. Iman started 2 seconds after Maya and finished 3 seconds before her. So Iman’s total time is 100 divided by x minus 5 seconds. We can set up the equation:

100 = 125 - 5 times 1.25 times x

Solve this to find x is 4 m/s, which is Maya’s speed.

Now, Iman’s total time for the race is 100 divided by 4 minus 5, which equals 20 seconds.
