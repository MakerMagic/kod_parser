Final Explanation

Divide pupils by teachers; both columns are in thousands, so the common scale cancels. Scotland has 893 ÷ 59 ≈ 15.14 pupils per teacher, below Northern Ireland's 341 ÷ 21 ≈ 16.24 and all the other regions. Therefore Scotland, D, has the lowest ratio.

  • A: England Midlands has 1613 ÷ 88 ≈ 18.33 pupils per teacher, greater than Scotland's 15.14.
  • B: England South has 2160 ÷ 121 ≈ 17.85 pupils per teacher, also higher than Scotland.
  • C: Wales has 497 ÷ 26 ≈ 19.12 pupils per teacher, the highest of these answer choices rather than the lowest.
  • E: Northern Ireland has fewer pupils in total but 341 ÷ 21 ≈ 16.24 per teacher, still above Scotland.
