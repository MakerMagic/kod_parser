Main Body

At the start of the school year, a village shop stocks and sells a range of stationery items. The table below shows details of the wholesale costs and retail prices for a number of the items sold.

Main Question

Of the five items sold, which two together made the most profit for the shop owner?

Main Answers

A

crayons and pencils

B

crayons and pens

C

felt tips and pencils

D

felt tips and rulers

E

pens and rulers

Correct Answer

B

Final Explanation

Divide wholesale pack costs by 12 before finding profit per item. Weekly profits are crayons (10−4)×180 = 1080p; felt tips (12−6)×150 = 900p; pencils (6−2)×200 = 800p; pens (15−3)×150 = 1800p; rulers (30−5)×40 = 1000p. Pens and crayons are the two largest, totalling £28.80: B.

  • A: Crayons and pencils yield £10.80+£8 = £18.80, below crayons and pens.
  • C: Felt tips and pencils yield £9+£8 = £17, well below £28.80.
  • D: Felt tips and rulers yield £9+£10 = £19, not the maximum.
  • E: Pens and rulers yield £18+£10 = £28, 80p less than pens and crayons.
