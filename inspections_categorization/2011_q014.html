Final Explanation

This question is simpler than it appears at first glance. Let the books be numbered 1-7, starting on the left (i.e. the left-most book will be Book 1, the second left-most book will be Book 2 and so forth, such that the right-most book will be Book 7).

If we look at Books 1, 2 and 3 under this system, we find that Book 1 is the shortest, Book 2 is taller than Book 1, and Book 3 is the tallest of these 3 books.

Thus, we know that any possible view from the left-most side (the direction of the arrow) must show a short book at the front, with a slightly taller book immediately behind it, and a taller yet book immediately behind this book.

We can see that in the view shown in C), there is a short book, followed by a much taller book immediately behind, and then a book which is between those 2 in height behind this one. Thus, these books cannot be Books 1, 2 and 3 as we observed in the front-view. Thus C) is not a possible view of the books, and C) is therefore the answer.
