Main Body

The following table summarises the results of all the matches played last season in a football league. (The home team’s score is always given first.) Each team plays every other team in the league twice; once at home and once away.

Main Question

Which was the only team that scored at least one goal in every match they played last season?

Main Answers

A

Amazon

B

Ganges

C

Nile

D

Rhine

E

Volga

Correct Answer

D

Final Explanation

A team's home goals are the first number in its row; its away goals are the second number in its column. Every non-diagonal home and away entry for Rhine is positive. The other listed teams each have at least one zero. Therefore Rhine, D, is the only listed team to score in every match.

  • A: Amazon loses 2–0 away to Nile, so Amazon scores no goals in that match.
  • B: Ganges loses 2–0 away to Yangtze; the second number is Ganges's score of zero.
  • C: Nile loses 1–0 away to Volga, leaving Nile with no goals.
  • E: Volga loses 1–0 away to Nile and therefore does not score in every match.
