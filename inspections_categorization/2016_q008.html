Final Explanation

These 3D non-verbal reasoning questions are often the hardest of TSA section 1. To solve this question, we will look for certain conditions imposed by the two views and rule out certain nets based on these conditions. Looking at the first view, we can see that the top of the letter A is adjacent to the bottom of letter B.
