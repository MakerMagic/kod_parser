Main Body

A picture 40cm high by 30cm wide is to be framed. There will be a mount between the edge of the picture and the frame. This mount will be 6cm wide at the top and sides, and 9cm wide at the bottom. The width of the wood used for the frame is 2cm.

Main Question

What is the overall height of the framed picture?

Main Answers

A

46cm

B

49cm

C

56cm

D

59cm

E

62cm

Correct Answer

D

Final Explanation

The overall height must include the picture itself, the mounting margins above and below it, and the wooden frame at both ends. The picture is 40 cm high, the top mount adds 6 cm, and the bottom mount adds 9 cm. The wooden frame contributes another 2 cm at the top and 2 cm at the bottom. Overall height = 40 + 6 + 9 + 2 + 2 = 59 cm. Therefore, D is correct.

  • A: 46 cm is smaller than the 55 cm required for the picture and its two mounting margins, even before adding the wooden frame.
  • B: 49 cm also fails to accommodate the combined 15 cm of top and bottom mounting around the 40 cm picture.
  • C: 56 cm is only 1 cm greater than the picture-and-mount height of 55 cm, whereas the wooden frame adds 4 cm.
  • E: 62 cm exceeds the established outer height by 3 cm. No additional component accounts for this difference.
