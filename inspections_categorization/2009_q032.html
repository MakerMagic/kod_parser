Boland City has just installed a new tram system. Several artists have been asked to come up with a map of the system which, whilst it may not show the exact relative positions of the stations, shows the connections between them correctly. The five maps are shown below (the names of the stations are not marked). Four of the artists drew possible maps, but the fifth got the connections wrong.

Main Question

Which map below is based on incorrect connections?
