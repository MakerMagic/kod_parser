Main Body

The table below shows the amount of waste recycled each year, in thousands of tonnes, over a three-year period in 20 countries.

Main Question

What is the biggest percentage increase in recycling in the table from year 1 to year 3?

Main Answers

A

50%

B

75%

C

100%

D

150%

E

200%

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

Percentage increase is (year 3−year 1)/year 1×100. Grandia rises from 20 to 60 thousand tonnes: (60−20)/20×100 = 200%. No other country triples its first-year total; the next largest increases include Carbonda's 100% and Killumpa's 75%. Therefore the largest increase is 200%, E.

  • A: 50% occurs for Nalpha, from 32 to 48, but is below Grandia's 200% increase.
  • B: 75% describes Killumpa's rise from 40 to 70, not the largest rise.
  • C: 100% describes Carbonda's doubling from 20 to 40; Grandia triples instead.
  • D: 150% would turn Grandia's 20 into 50, whereas the table shows 60.
