Final Explanation

Subtract each 2012 figure from the corresponding 2007 figure. From January to December the differences are 37.1, 10.1, 77.4, 29.9, 25.8, 35.1, 33.6, 18.7, 60.1, 18.3, 10.2 and 16.7. March must be tallest, September second, and January slightly higher than June and July. Chart D also preserves the smaller late-year values, so D is correct.

  • A: Its September bar is taller than March's, whereas the differences are 60.1 and 77.4 respectively.
  • B: Its January bar is too low relative to June and July: January's 37.1 should exceed both 35.1 and 33.6.
  • C: It makes March and September approximately equal and exaggerates several smaller months; their actual differences are distinct.
  • E: It puts September above March and makes several small differences too large, contrary to the calculated monthly pattern.
