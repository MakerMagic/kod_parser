Final Explanation

Tax paid is annual income multiplied by the stated rate. Tim pays $10000×30% = $3000. David pays $50000×6% = $3000 as well, so C is correct. Matching the rate alone would be insufficient because the incomes differ.

  • A: Neil pays $20000×25% = $5000, which is $2000 more than Tim.
  • B: Veronica pays $150000×3% = $4500, not $3000 despite her lower rate.
  • D: Amy pays $80000×5% = $4000, exceeding Tim's tax by $1000.
  • E: Roz has Tim's 30% rate but pays it on $12000, giving $3600 rather than $3000.
