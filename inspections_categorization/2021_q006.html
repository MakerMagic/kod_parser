Main Body

I have received the following message that is encrypted with a Caesar cipher: JYMDWEFTQEBAF This Caesar cipher replaces each letter of the plaintext (the message to be encrypted) with a letter that is a certain fixed number of positions along the 26-letter English alphabet. Knowing this fixed number of positions (the shift parameter) is the key to decrypt the message. For example, if the letter in the plaintext is A (the 1st letter of the alphabet) and the shift parameter is 4, then the encrypted letter is E (the 5th letter of the alphabet). Similarly, with a shift parameter of 7, Y (the 25th letter of the alphabet) is encrypted as F, which is the 32nd letter if the alphabet is repeated immediately after Z. I would like to decrypt the message that I have received but I do not know the shift parameter. I only know that the first letter of the plaintext is X.

Main Question

What is the sixth letter of the decrypted message?

Final Explanation

The shift parameter is 14 (J to X) so the E becomes S
