Main Body

Anna buys potatoes in the local market. She has noticed that the price on Saturday morning is 5p per kilo more than the normal weekday price. However, an hour before the stall closes on a Saturday afternoon, the price drops to 5p per kilo below the weekday price. She spends £3.00 a week on potatoes. This will buy her 3 kilos less in weight on a Saturday morning than on a weekday, but 5 kilos more in weight at the low Saturday afternoon price than on a weekday.

Main Question

What is the weekday price of potatoes per kilo?

Main Answers

A

12p

B

15p

C

20p

D

25p

E

30p

Correct Answer

C
