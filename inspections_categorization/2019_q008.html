Main Body

The bar chart shows the total numbers of tickets sold for a concert over three nights. number of tickets sold

Premium tickets are more expensive than Standard tickets.

Main Question

Which one of the following statements is correct?

Main Answers

A

The highest total sales income was for Thursday.

B

The highest total sales income was for Friday.

C

The highest total sales income could have been for Thursday or Friday.

D

The highest total sales income could have been for Thursday or Saturday.

E

The highest total sales income could have been for Friday or Saturday.

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

Let Standard cost s and Premium cost p, with p>s. Friday earns 150s+225 p, exceeding Thursday's 200s+150 p by 75 p−50s, which is positive. Saturday earns 300s+125 p; compared with Friday its advantage is 150s−100 p. This can be positive or negative depending on whether p is below or above 1.5s. Thus Friday or Saturday could have the highest income: E.

  • A: Thursday cannot be highest because Friday necessarily earns more whenever Premium is dearer than Standard.
  • B: Friday is not guaranteed highest: with s=1 and p=1.2, Saturday earns 450 versus Friday's 420.
  • C: Thursday is impossible as the highest-income night, so the proposed Thursday-or-Friday alternatives are wrong.
  • D: This also includes impossible Thursday and omits Friday, which can be highest when Premium is sufficiently expensive.
