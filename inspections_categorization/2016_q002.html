Main Body

An unusual dartboard is used in a playground game:

In order to win the big prize, contestants must score exactly 50 with three darts, all of which must be in different sectors.

Main Question

Which number must they hit with one of the three darts in order to score 50?

Main Answers

A

8

B

11

C

18

D

21

E

24

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

The quickest way to do this is to find some solutions and look for a common number. Working modulo 2, it is obvious that we need to

select either 2 odd numbers and 1 even number or all 3 even numbers. This gives us the following valid combination (8,18,24) and

(18,11,21). The answer is therefore C.
