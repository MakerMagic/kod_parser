Main Body

A company is producing a one-page flysheet 24cm high by 18cm wide. It will have a text area in the centre which has a margin of the same width all the way around. The text must occupy exactly half of the total area:

Main Question

What is the width of the margin?

Main Answers

A

3.00cm

B

4.50cm

C

5.25cm

D

6.00cm

E

12.00cm

Correct Answer

A

Final Explanation

Let the length of the margin be X. Then we can form a quadratic equation and solve for X:

Since X=18cm is not a valid solution, we have that the margin is 3cm.
