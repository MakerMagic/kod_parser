Main Body

A manufacturer wishes to make an open-topped box out of the piece of cardboard shown below by folding up its sides.

Main Question

What is the volume of this box in cubic centimetres?

Main Answers

A

1 600

B

2 400

C

8 000

D

10 125

E

12 500

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

If 5cm is folded up on each side, the box is 40cm by 40cm on the bottom (50cm minus two lots of 5cm). It is 5cm high. Hence the volume is 5 x 40x40cm, which is 8000cm3. Hence the answer is C.
