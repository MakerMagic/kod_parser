Main Body

There are 24 pupils in a class. They decided to help the local park by planting a total of 24 plants, a mixture of birch trees and roses. Each girl planted three roses, and every three boys planted one birch tree between them.

Main Question

How many more boys than girls are there in the class?

Main Answers

A

3

B

6

C

8

D

12

E

18

Correct Answer

D

Final Explanation

Let g be the number of girls and b the number of boys. Since there are 24 pupils, g + b = 24. Each girl plants 3 roses, while every 3 boys collectively plant 1 birch tree, so the total number of plants is 3g + b/3 = 24. Multiplying the second equation by 3 gives 9g + b = 72. Subtracting g + b = 24 gives 8g = 48, so g = 6. Therefore, b = 24 − 6 = 18. The difference is 18 − 6 = 12 pupils. Therefore, D is correct.

  • A: A difference of 3 would require 13.5 boys and 10.5 girls, which is impossible because pupil counts must be whole numbers.
  • B: A difference of 6 would mean 15 boys and 9 girls. They would plant 5 birch trees and 27 roses, giving 32 plants rather than 24.
  • C: A difference of 8 would mean 16 boys and 8 girls. The girls alone would plant all 24 plants, leaving no allowance for the boys' birch trees.
  • E: A difference of 18 would mean 21 boys and 3 girls. They would plant 7 birch trees and 9 roses, totalling only 16 plants.
