Main Body

Hockey is an 11-a-side game, but a team may consist of up to 16 players, with unlimited substitutions allowed throughout a match of 70 minutes duration. Roger captains the Buccaneers hockey team. He has 16 players for today's match. He will play the whole match himself, as will the goalkeeper. He intends to rotate all the others in such a way that each of them spends the same total amount of time on the pitch.

Main Question

How much time should each player (except Roger and the goalkeeper) spend on the pitch during today's match?

Main Answers

A

39 3/8 minutes

B

45 minutes

C

46 2/3 minutes

D

48 1/8 minutes

E

55 minutes

Correct Answer

B

Final Explanation

If Roger and the goalkeeper play the whole match, then there are 9 players on the pitch who are ones who are being “rotated”. If there are 16 players in total, without Roger and the goalkeeper this is 14 players. Hence each player will play 9/14 of the whole match, which is 70 minutes. 1/14 of a 70 minute match is 5 minutes as 70 divided by 14 is 5. Hence 9/14 of the match is 45 minutes. Hence the answer is B.
