Main Body

The heptathlon is a seven-event athletics competition. In each event the time or distance recorded is converted into a points score. This is the scoring table that is used for the high jump:

Daphne is competing in a heptathlon competition and has just beaten her previous best high jump performance by 18cm. This has increased her best points score in the high jump by 214.

Main Question

What is Daphne’s new personal-best high jump performance?

Main Answers

A

1.09 metres

B

1.44 metres

C

1.74 metres

D

1.89 metres

E

2.09 metres

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

For each proposed new height, compare its points with the score 0.18 m lower. At 1.74 m the table gives 903 points; at 1.56 m it gives 689. The increase is 903 − 689 = 214, exactly as stated. Therefore Daphne's new best is 1.74 m, C.

  • A: A new height of 1.09 m would mean an old height of 0.91 m, below the table's lowest listed height; it cannot supply the required pair of table entries.
  • B: At 1.44 m the score is 555, compared with 369 at 1.26 m: an increase of 186, not 214.
  • D: At 1.89 m the score is 1093, compared with 867 at 1.71 m: an increase of 226.
  • E: At 2.09 m the score is 1359, compared with 1119 at 1.91 m: an increase of 240.
