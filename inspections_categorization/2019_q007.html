Main Body

The table below shows the breakdown of marks awarded to students taking an exam.

The mark scheme is as follows: 90 = excellent; 80 = good; 70 = satisfactory; 60 = acceptable; 50 = fail. The final mark is the average between the marks given by the first and second examiners.

Main Question

Which pair of examiners disagreed the most on the marks they awarded a student?

Main Answers

A

Mr Kettering and Mrs De Vere

B

Mr Robson and Ms Nolan

C

Ms Rosenberg and Ms Nolan

D

Mr Kettering and Ms Rosenberg

E

Mrs De Vere and Mr Robson

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

Compare the absolute difference between each student's two marks. Hillary Gordon receives 50 from Mr Robson and 90 from Mrs De Vere, a difference of 40. This exceeds Betty's 30 and every other difference, which is at most 10. Therefore Mrs De Vere and Mr Robson, E, disagree most.

  • A: Mr Kettering and Mrs De Vere differ by 30 on Betty's marks, below the maximum difference of 40.
  • B: Mr Robson and Ms Nolan differ by only 10 on Clara's marks, 50 versus 60.
  • C: Ms Rosenberg and Ms Nolan differ by 10 on Janine's marks, 80 versus 90.
  • D: Mr Kettering and Ms Rosenberg both give Cora 80, so they do not disagree on her mark.
