Main Body

Nathan lives in London. His older brother Mark lives in San Diego, his younger brother Ben lives in Barcelona and his sister Isabel lives in Nairobi. Nathan is trying to arrange an online call between all four siblings. Each of the siblings has agreed to be available between 08:00 and 20:00 local time. San Diego is 8 hours behind London time. Barcelona is 1 hour ahead of London time. Nairobi is 2 hours ahead of London time.

Main Question

For how many hours during a 24-hour period are at least three of the four siblings able to be online at the same time?

Main Answers

8

B

9

C

10

D

11

E

12

Correct Answer

D

Final Explanation

Convert everyone's availability into London time. Nathan is available 08:00–20:00, Mark 16:00–04:00 the next day, Ben 07:00–19:00, and Isabel 06:00–18:00. From 08:00 to 16:00, Nathan, Ben and Isabel are available together: 8 hours. From 16:00 to 18:00, all four siblings are available: 2 hours. From 18:00 to 19:00, Nathan, Mark and Ben are available: 1 hour. Outside these intervals, fewer than three siblings are available simultaneously. Therefore, the total is 8 + 2 + 1 = 11 hours, making D correct.

  • A: 8 hours counts only the overlap from 08:00 to 16:00, excluding the three additional hours when Mark becomes available.
  • B: 9 hours is two hours short of the established total across the three qualifying intervals.
  • C: 10 hours excludes one of the 11 qualifying hours, including the additional overlap from 18:00 to 19:00 when three siblings remain available.
  • E: 12 hours exceeds the total by one hour. At 19:00 Ben becomes unavailable, leaving only Nathan and Mark online until 20:00.
