Main Body

The mock examination and actual GCSE results for English in one year at Morgan College are shown below:

Main Question

What percentage of pupils had their GCSE results correctly predicted by their mock examination results?

Main Answers

A

25.0 %

B

50.0 %

C

57.5 %

D

60.0 %

E

75.0 %

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

There are 200 pupils altogether. 90 got A-C in their mock exams and then again in their actual exams, so this was correctly predicted. Another 60 got other grades in their mock exams and then again in their actual exams, so this was correctly predicted.

Hence for 150 of the 200 pupils, the mocks correctly predicted their GCSE results. This is equal to 75% of the pupils.

Hence the answer is E.
