Main Body

Julie lives in London. She has three daughters; April, who lives in New York (where the local time is 5 hours behind London), May, who lives in Vancouver (8 hours behind London), and June, who lives in Tokyo ( 9 hours ahead of London). They all communicate with each other regularly. Last week April received a text message from May that said "call me on Wednesday at 23.30 your time." Unfortunately, the message had been sent to April by mistake and was intended for June.

Main Question

How much later than expected did May's phone eventually ring?

Main Answers

A

12 hours

B

13 hours

C

14 hours

D

17 hours

E

22 hours

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

The question is how far behind June’s time zone April lives, because we want to know how much later April thinks 23:30 on Wednesday is than June. April lives 5 hours behind London time and June lives 9 hours ahead. Hence the total time differences is 14 hours. Hence the answer is C.
