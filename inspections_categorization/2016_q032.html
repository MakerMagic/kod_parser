Final Explanation

I will refer to the graphs as 1-4 with graph 1 being on the left and graph 4 being on the right. It is clear that the regular structure of graph 1 lends itself to container C. Similarly, the two part linear structure of graph 3 lends itself to container E. Graph 2 has a linear portion followed by a portion with decreasing gradient.
