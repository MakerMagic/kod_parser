Main Body

The Head of Year 6 is planning an end-of-term treat for her students. The pie chart represents the numbers of votes cast by the children for each of the five possible outings.

Main Question

Which one of the following rows (A–E) could show the actual number of votes cast by the children for each of the options?

Main Answers

A

35 45 30 15 55 180

B

24 28 20 16 32 120

C

45 55 20 15 65 200

D

22 28 15 15 40 120

E

32 36 20 14 48 150

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

Compare proportions, not raw vote totals. The chart's sectors are approximately train 21%, adventure 24%, farm 13%, museum 9% and theme park 32%. Row E gives 32/150≈21.3%, 36/150=24%, 20/150≈13.3%, 14/150≈9.3% and 48/150=32%, matching those sector sizes. Therefore E could represent the votes.

  • A: Its farm share is 30/180=16.7%, making a 60-degree sector, noticeably larger than the pictured farm sector of about 48 degrees.
  • B: Its museum share is 16/120=13.3%, too large for the smallest pictured sector of about 9%.
  • C: Its farm share is 20/200=10%, too small for the pictured farm sector, which is about 13%.
  • D: It assigns 15 votes each to farm and museum, but their sectors are visibly unequal.
