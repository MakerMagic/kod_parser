Main Body

The sales for the four departments of a store are shown in the two pie charts below.

Main Question

Which one of the following lists the departments in order, from highest average amount spent per customer to lowest?

Main Answers

A

clothes, furniture, stationery, food

B

food, clothes, stationery, furniture

C

food, stationery, clothes, furniture

D

food, stationery, furniture, clothes

E

furniture, clothes, stationery, food

Correct Answer

E

Final Explanation

You need to compare the sales to the number of customers - the area that has the highest ratio of area of sales to customers is first, and the area with the smallest ratio of area of sales to customers is last. Therefore furniture is first, then clothes (both have more sale area than customers area) then stationery which is roughly equal, then food.
