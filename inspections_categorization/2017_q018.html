Main Body

I am planning to repaint the walls and the ceiling of my dining room. The room has the shape of a rectangular box and is 8m long, 4m wide and 3m high. I do not need to paint the door and 2 the three windows, the combined surface area of which is 10m . Paint is sold in 8-litre tins and 2 1 litre of paint is sufficient for a surface area of 12m .

Main Question

How many tins of paint do I need to buy?

D

8

Correct Answer

A

Final Explanation

The room requires painting on four walls and the ceiling, excluding the door and windows. Wall area = 2(8 × 3) + 2(4 × 3) = 72 m². Ceiling area = 8 × 4 = 32 m². Subtracting the 10 m² occupied by the door and windows gives 72 + 32 − 10 = 94 m². Each litre covers 12 m², so the required paint is 94 ÷ 12 ≈ 7.83 litres. One 8-litre tin covers 96 m², which is sufficient. Therefore, A is correct: one tin.

  • B: Two tins contain 16 litres, whereas the calculated requirement is less than 8 litres.
  • C: Six tins provide 48 litres, far beyond the amount required to cover the 94 m² paintable area.
  • D: Eight tins would cover 768 m², substantially exceeding the area requiring paint.
  • E: Seventeen tins provide 136 litres, sufficient to cover 1,632 m², greatly exceeding the calculated requirement.
