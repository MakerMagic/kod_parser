Main Body

The following table compares five selected commuting routes in different European countries with respect to the length of the journey and the monthly cost of a season ticket as a percentage of average monthly wages.

Main Question

On average, the commuters of which route spend the lowest proportion of their wages per kilometre of the route?

Main Answers

A

Luton–London

B

Brandenburg–Berlin

C

Karis–Helsinki

D

Uppsala–Stockholm

E

Drogheda–Dublin

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

Divide each wage percentage by route length. Karis–Helsinki costs 6/75 = 0.08 percentage points of wages per kilometre. Uppsala–Stockholm has the same 6% total cost but a shorter 72 km route, so its per-kilometre proportion is higher. All the other ratios also exceed 0.08. Therefore C is correct.

  • A: Luton–London gives 14/56 = 0.25 percentage points per kilometre, much higher than 0.08.
  • B: Brandenburg–Berlin gives 8/69 ≈ 0.116, above the Finnish route's ratio.
  • D: Uppsala–Stockholm gives 6/72 ≈ 0.0833, slightly above 6/75.
  • E: Drogheda–Dublin's lowest total percentage is spread over only 46 km: 5/46 ≈ 0.109, still higher.
