Main Body

Fiona is going to put some shelves up in her living room. The shelves must each be 1.80m long and have a depth of at least 40cm. There will be five shelves in total. The different prices for wood are shown in the table below.

Fiona can cut pieces of wood to make more than one piece of the correct size, but she wants each of the shelves to be made of one complete piece of wood.

Main Question

What is the cheapest total cost for which Fiona can make her shelves?

Main Answers

A

$21.15

B

$23.25

C

$23.55

D

$24.75

E

$25.50

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

Shelves must be single pieces at least 40 cm deep, so 30 cm boards cannot be used. A 4 m board at 45 cm depth costs $9.30 and gives two 1.8 m shelves; a 2 m board costs $4.95 and gives one. Buy two 4 m boards and one 2 m board: 2×$9.30+$4.95 = $23.55 for five shelves. This is C.

  • A: $21.15 is below the minimum: each pair of complete shelves costs at least $9.30 and the remaining single shelf at least $4.95.
  • B: $23.25 is 30 cents below that verified minimum; shorter offcuts cannot be joined into a complete shelf.
  • D: $24.75 buys five separate 2 m boards at $4.95 each but misses the saving from two 4 m boards.
  • E: $25.50 buys the analogous 60 cm-deep boards, which are more expensive than the sufficient 45 cm depth.
