Full Explanation

The builder has 32 kg of a mix made up of 8 kg of sand (25%) and therefore 24 kg of cement. He needs his final mix to be 120 kg, made up of 80 kg of sand and 40 kg of cement (2 : 1 ratio). This means that he needs to add 72 kg of sand and 16 kg of cement to his original mix. This is a ratio of 72 : 16, which, in simplest terms, is 9 : 2. The correct answer is option D.
