Main Body

Car parking charges are shown in the table below. Brian wants to park his car for a period of 7.5 hours, whilst he is at work. He buys a ticket in the morning when he arrives at the car park. Due to the close proximity of the car park to his place of work, he is able to return to his car to buy a new ticket as many times as needed.

Main Question

What is the most Brian can save by returning to the car to buy new tickets compared to buying one 8-hour ticket?

Main Answers

A

£1.10

B

£1.40

C

£1.60

D

£2.10

E

£3.60

Correct Answer

C

Final Explanation

An 8-hour ticket costs £6.20. Two 3-hour tickets and a 2-hour ticket cover the required 7.5 hours for £1.70+£1.70+£1.20 = £4.60. Comparing combinations of the listed whole-hour allowances shows none covering at least eight hours costs less. The maximum saving is £6.20−£4.60 = £1.60, C.

  • A: A £1.10 saving means paying £5.10, more than the available £4.60 combination.
  • B: A £1.40 saving is possible with four 2-hour tickets costing £4.80, but it is not the greatest saving.
  • D: A £2.10 saving would require an adequate combination costing only £4.10; the cheapest covering combination is £4.60.
  • E: A £3.60 saving would mean paying £2.60, insufficient to cover 7.5 hours under the listed tariffs.
