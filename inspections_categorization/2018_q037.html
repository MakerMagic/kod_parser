Main Body

There is an automatic photograph booth in my local shopping centre. It takes exactly 2 minutes from the time that the money is inserted for the photographs to be taken, then the developed photographs appear 4 minutes later. Money may only be inserted when the green light is on. This light goes out immediately after money is inserted, and comes back on again exactly 3 minutes later. This morning, I arrived at the booth just as it was being switched on, and I was sixth in the queue. The first person inserted their money immediately, and the rest of us all inserted our money as soon as we were allowed to.

Main Question

How long after I arrived at the booth did I insert my money?

Main Answers

A

15 minutes

B

18 minutes

C

21 minutes

D

25 minutes

E

30 minutes

Correct Answer

A

Final Explanation

The green light returns exactly 3 minutes after money is inserted, so consecutive customers can pay at 3-minute intervals. The first person inserts money immediately upon the booth opening, at 0 minutes. As sixth in the queue, the narrator must wait for five earlier customers to insert their money. Their payment times are 0, 3, 6, 9 and 12 minutes. The narrator can therefore insert money at 5 × 3 = 15 minutes after arrival. The photograph-taking and development times do not change the stated payment interval. Therefore, A is correct.

  • B: 18 minutes is when the seventh customer could insert money. The narrator is sixth and therefore pays at 15 minutes.
  • C: 21 minutes corresponds to the eighth payment opportunity, two positions after the narrator's.
  • D: 25 minutes is not one of the permitted payment times, which occur at multiples of 3 minutes from the booth opening.
  • E: 30 minutes corresponds to the eleventh payment opportunity, five places later than the narrator's position.
