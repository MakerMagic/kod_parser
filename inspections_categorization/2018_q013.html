Main Body

I am redecorating my house and need to buy wallpaper for one of the rooms. I need to buy 54m of wallpaper in total and want to pay the lowest price. The table below shows the details of the five types of wallpaper I want to choose between.

Main Question

Which type of wallpaper should I choose?

Main Answers

A

woodchip

B

vymura

C

anaglypta

D

embossed

E

fabric effect

Correct Answer

D

Final Explanation

Enough whole rolls must be bought to supply at least 54 m. Embossed needs 14 rolls of 4 m; its 25% discount gives 14×£50×0.75 = £525. The comparable totals are woodchip £550, vymura £567, anaglypta £576 and fabric effect £600. Embossed, D, is cheapest.

  • A: Woodchip needs 27 rolls. Five are free under every-fifth-roll-free, leaving 22×£25 = £550, £25 more than embossed.
  • B: Vymura needs 18 rolls: 18×£35×0.9 = £567 after its qualifying discount.
  • C: Anaglypta also needs 18 rolls, costing 18×£40×0.8 = £576 after discount.
  • E: Fabric effect needs 11 rolls, so six paid rolls at £100 each are required under buy-one-get-one-free: £600.
