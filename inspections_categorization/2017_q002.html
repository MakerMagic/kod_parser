Main Body

A café owner makes 50 mini pizzas and 50 flapjacks for sale during the day. The pizzas are sold for $2.00 each and the flapjacks are sold for $1.00 each. However, by the end of lunchtime there are 10 mini pizzas still unsold and 15 flapjacks unsold, so the price of each is halved for the afternoon. At the end of the day there are still 2 mini pizzas and 1 flapjack left which are given away free to the last customer.

Main Question

If the original 50 mini pizzas and 50 flapjacks had a combined production cost of $30.00, how much profit is made on the sale of the mini pizzas and flapjacks for the day?

Main Answers

A

$100.00

B

$102.50

C

$115.00

D

$120.00

E

$130.00

Correct Answer

A

Final Explanation

Profit equals total sales revenue minus production cost. Before lunchtime ends, 40 pizzas sell for $2 each and 35 flapjacks for $1 each. In the afternoon, 8 pizzas sell for $1 each and 14 flapjacks for $0.50 each. The remaining 2 pizzas and 1 flapjack generate no revenue because they are given away. Total revenue = (40 × 2) + (35 × 1) + (8 × 1) + (14 × 0.50) = $130. Subtracting the $30 production cost gives $130 − $30 = $100. Therefore, A is correct.

  • B: $102.50 exceeds the calculated profit by $2.50. The stated sales and production cost establish a profit of exactly $100.
  • C: $115 exceeds the calculated profit by $15 and does not account consistently for the established revenue and production cost.
  • D: $120 is $20 greater than the calculated profit, despite the total revenue being only $130 and production costing $30.
  • E: $130 is the total sales revenue, not the profit. The $30 production cost must still be deducted.
